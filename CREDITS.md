## Original game assets by Michael Wilmes

All other icons, plus the jordie, qbird, snuggles, crystal, bandit and blobs assets, the Puffin monster photo (`puffin.png`, photographed by Michael Wilmes), the victory music `Fight End.mp3` (2006), the defeat music `Funeral March.mp3` (Mike's own recording of Chopin's public-domain Funeral March, made in Waveform 13), and these sounds made in QUACK Sound Effects Studio (patch files dated 2005): `hi_swish.mp3` (HiSwish.pm), `cast_2.mp3` (Blobs Cast 2.pm), `fireball.mp3` (Fireball.pm), `skill.mp3` (Skill.pm), `electrocute3.mp3` (Electric Shockwave; Electrocute/Electrocute2.pm).

These were made procedurally for Dragon Star in October 2026, using the scripts in `utils/asset-generators/`. No third-party samples or artwork were used. They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
